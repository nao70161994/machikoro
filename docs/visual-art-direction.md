# Visual art direction

## Reference findings

Official Machi Koro product pages show establishment and landmark cards, and Grounding says the 2025 edition changed card size and design. The useful reference is the way each card makes its game information easy to scan:

- Dice result and card type are visible at the top.
- A distinct illustration of the establishment occupies the center.
- Name, cost, and effect sit together at the bottom.
- Card color communicates the activation family, so players can scan a market before reading every effect.

References: [Grounding, Machi Koro (2025 Japanese edition)](https://www.g-rounding.com/board-game/machikoro_sinsou/) and [Asmodee, Machi Koro product images](https://store.asmodee.com/products/machi-koro). Grounding explicitly notes the 2025 edition changed card size and design; Asmodee's US product gallery is an earlier Pandasaurus edition and includes establishment/landmark card photos. Treat those editions as separate visual references: compare information hierarchy and quick category/effect scanning, but do not infer current card proportions from the older photos. Keep Dice City's dusk city, frames, typography, and illustrations original.

Use these as information-design references. Do not reuse their card frames, illustrations, logos, or other protected art. Dice City keeps its own title, card layout, palette, illustrations, and city imagery.

## Dice City direction

Build a charming illustrated city at dusk: deep blue-green surroundings, warm sunset light, pale paper-like card faces, and small architectural details that reward a closer look. The art should feel like one illustrated town across title, market, player town, and result screens.

- Make each facility illustration recognizable by its subject before adding decoration. Give every one of the 38 facilities its own silhouette and a small set of defining details.
- Use a consistent hand-drawn vector language: confident dark outlines, rounded joins, a limited palette, clear foreground and background planes, and restrained highlights and cast shadows.
- Keep card activation colors distinct and consistent with the game rules. Use the color as a quick category cue while preserving readable text and contrast.
- Make the illustration the visual focus of a facility card. Keep dice, category, name, cost, and effect legible at the 390px phone layout and at desktop market widths.
- Carry the same dusk lighting, outline treatment, and material palette into the town miniature and winner screen. Avoid platform emoji as major decorative art.
- Keep the existing classic design available and visually separate; the sunset direction must not change game rules or saved data.
